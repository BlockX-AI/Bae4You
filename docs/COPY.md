# Copy Guidelines — Catch Up

## 1. Tone of Voice

Direct, specific, plain English. We assume the user is literate in trading and social apps, so we skip explanations they already know. No onboarding poetry. No brand manifestos. Every word should move the user toward a decision: buy, sell, hold, or research.

We write in the imperative for CTAs, declarative for data, and avoid the continuous/present-progressive tense ("is loading" becomes "Loading...", "is updating" becomes "Updated just now"). Active voice, short sentences, no adjectives without quantifiers.

---

## 2. Vocabulary

### Approved Terms
- **Pets** — the core asset. "I own 12 pets."
- **Pets Cash / PCASH** — the soft currency. Always written "PCASH" in UI, "Pets Cash" in full sentences.
- **Value** — current market price of a pet.
- **Assets** — collective term for pets owned.
- **Collection** — a user's portfolio of pets.
- **Wishlist** — saved pets to track or buy later.
- **Ranked** — position on leaderboard.
- **Lock** — unavailable for trade (cooldown, escrow, or self-imposed).
- **Owner** — current holder of a pet.

### Forbidden Terms
Do not use unless directly, factually relevant:
- asset class, investment opportunity, financial instrument
- journey, revolutionize, paradigm shift, web3-native, on-chain experience
- empower, unlock, elevate, level up, transform
- ecosystem, platform, solution, offering

## 6. Error Messages

1. **Transaction failed.** Insufficient PCASH. Add funds or reduce bid amount.
2. **Pet unavailable.** Sold or locked while you were bidding. Refresh to see current status.
3. **Connection lost.** Check your network. Changes auto-save when you reconnect.
4. **Bid too low.** Minimum bid is PCASH 500. Increase to place.
5. **Account restricted.** Contact support if you believe this is an error.
